    Welcome to the card room

    A portfolio you can walk around

    I'm Eyal, a founding engineer at Arkero and a student at the University of Washington. Everything I've built is in this room, and you play as my dog.

    Wander the room, take a seat, and the people and things in it will tell you about my work. It's all hand-built in the browser with Three.js.

    What's in the room

    Six places to stop

    ♠The poker tableTake the empty chair. Every player is a project I built and deals you its story. Fold, call, raise, or go all in.
    ♥Chess with EyalBotA neural net trained on 6,606 of my real chess.com games. It runs in your browser and plays like me, blunders included.
    ♦The libraryPull a book off the shelf to read my essays, page by page.
    ♣The soccer wallPhotos and highlight videos from club soccer in the ECNL and my high school team.
    ♠The barOrder my pint. The bartender knows what I'd have.
    ♦The Music CornerSit at the grand piano and play the keys, or pick up the guitar and strum some chords.
    ♥16 chipsEach place you discover earns a chip. See if you can find them all.
